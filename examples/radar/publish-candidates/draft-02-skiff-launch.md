# Skiff's next release has a quarter, not a date

Skiff opened a waitlist for its next release. The announcement gives a quarter
and no date.

## Why this is a draft and not a publish

The last two Skiff announcements slipped by roughly a month each. Writing up a
window that has moved twice is how a publication trains its readers to ignore
it.

## What would make this publishable

- A date, not a quarter.
- A feature list rather than a theme.
- Any independent confirmation that the build exists.

Until one of those lands, this sits here.
