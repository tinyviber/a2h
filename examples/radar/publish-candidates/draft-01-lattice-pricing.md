# Lattice cut its mid-tier price by 60% and did not announce it

Lattice's price sheet changed this week. The mid-tier model now costs 40% of
what it did last quarter for the same context window — and there was no
announcement, no blog post, and no changelog entry until this morning.

## What changed

- Mid-tier list price: down 60% quarter over quarter.
- Context window: unchanged.
- Small tier: unchanged, which is the odd part.

## Why the pattern matters

Price cuts usually land on the cheap model first — that is where volume is. A
cut that skips the small tier and targets the middle suggests Lattice is
defending against something specific rather than chasing volume.

The sheet is dated the 8th. The changelog entry is dated today. Either the
sheet shipped early, or the changelog is late.

## What to watch

Whether the small tier follows within a fortnight. If it does not, this was a
targeted move rather than a general repricing.
