# Lattice drops inference pricing again

**Feed:** lattice-blog · **Received:** 2026-09-10T07:00:03Z · **Item:** 12

Lattice published a new price sheet this morning. The headline: their mid-tier
model is now billed at 40% of last quarter's rate for the same context window.

Two things make this worth a second look rather than a headline:

1. The cut lands only on the mid tier. The small model is unchanged, which is
   the opposite of the usual pattern.
2. The price sheet is dated the 8th but the changelog entry is dated today.
   Either the sheet shipped early or the changelog is late.

No announcement post accompanies the change. That silence is itself the signal.
